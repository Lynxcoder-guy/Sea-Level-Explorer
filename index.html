Sea Level and their Creatures

Welcome Sea Explorer

Welcome to ocean exploration! Did you know that over 80% of our planet's oceans remain mysterious and unexplored? As we deepen, pressure increases and sunlight gradually fades. From the bright surface to the pitch-black depths of the deepest trenches, each depth level harbors unique ecosystems and extraordinary creatures adapted to survive in extreme conditions. Let's dive deeper and discover who inhabits each of our ocean's deepest zones.
